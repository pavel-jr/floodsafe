Regularized logistic regression predicts catalog flood severity, conditional on an assumed flood. It does not predict contamination or flood occurrence. This classifier does not contribute to the simulated sampling score.

Method and limitations

Inputs: latitude, longitude, month, country and flood type. Duplicate Israel event IDs removed. Evaluation: train before 2018, test from 2018 onward. Final model refitted on all 38 flood events. No casualties, narratives or post-event damage used as features. Small curated sample; no non-event controls; geographic generalization and probability calibration unvalidated. Zero correct Moderate or Very High test classifications. Source claims not independently verified.

Scenario formula: F = min(100, initial flood exposure × multiplier); priority = (0.35F + 0.30S + 0.20W + 0.15P) × F/100. Weights and thresholds are assumptions.
